# Purpose

This document defines the success metrics for MuscleX.

Every feature released into production must positively impact one or more of the metrics defined here.

Metrics exist to improve users' lives—not simply to increase usage.

---

# Product Philosophy

MuscleX does not measure success by:

❌ Downloads

❌ App Installs

❌ Registered Accounts

❌ Revenue Alone

Instead we measure:

✔ Healthy Habits

✔ Consistency

✔ User Success

✔ Goal Achievement

✔ Daily Value

✔ Long-Term Retention

---

# Primary North Star Metric

## Weekly Active Healthy Users (WAHU)

### Definition

A Weekly Active Healthy User is a person who performs meaningful health activities during a rolling seven-day period.

Examples include:

• Completing a workout

• Logging meals

• Tracking water intake

• Recording body measurements

• Completing a challenge

• Following an AI recommendation

• Checking into a gym

• Logging recovery

• Completing sleep tracking

Simply opening the app does not qualify.

---

# Why WAHU?

WAHU measures meaningful engagement rather than passive usage.

It reflects whether users are actually improving their health.

---

# Feature Evaluation Framework

Before shipping any feature ask:

Will this improve at least one KPI?

YES

↓

Build

NO

↓

Reject or Redesign

---

# Decision Framework

If a feature has:

Low Usage

Low Retention

High Complexity

High Maintenance

No Strategic Value

It should be redesigned or removed.
