# Purpose

This document identifies the real-world problems users experience throughout their health and fitness journey.

Every feature in MuscleX should solve one or more documented pain points.

This document serves as the foundation for:

• Product Features

• UX Decisions

• AI Recommendations

• User Flows

• Notifications

• Design System

• Roadmap Prioritization

---

# Product Rules

Every feature request must reference:

At least one Pain Point

AND

At least one JTBD

AND

At least one Business Goal

If not,

the feature should be rejected or redesigned.

---

# Prioritization Matrix

Critical

Prevents users from achieving goals.

High

Significantly improves user experience.

Medium

Improves efficiency or delight.

Low

Nice-to-have enhancements.

---

# Success Metrics

Success is measured by reductions in:

- User confusion
- Onboarding abandonment
- Workout drop-off
- Meal logging abandonment
- Membership churn
- Support tickets
- Notification opt-outs

And increases in:

- Retention
- Daily engagement
- Workout completion
- Nutrition consistency
- Recovery adherence
- User satisfaction
- App Store ratings
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

---

# Product Principle

Pain points are opportunities.

Every frustration we remove creates trust.

Every moment we simplify creates loyalty.

Every improvement we make helps someone build a healthier life.
